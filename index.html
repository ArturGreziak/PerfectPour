How to Play

Hold the play area, the Pour button or Space to pour. Water falls from the fixed nozzle into a moving cup.

Release early: drops take time to reach the cup. Wait for the stream to settle, then tap Check Pour.

Match the gold line within the displayed tolerance. Overfill, 29 missed drops or 28 seconds ends the round.

Finish 30 levels. Pause freezes the game. Progress saves automatically.
